Plateia AI is a grassroots, independent initiative committed to making artificial intelligence accessible, inclusive, and transparent for everyone in Greece. Born from a collective effort of researchers, educators, and citizens, Plateia AI aims to bridge the gap between technology and society by fostering open dialogue, education, and collaboration. Our digital platform serves as a public space where people can explore AI developments, connect with others, and contribute to a shared understanding of how AI shapes our world.
