About

Born from the collective effort of researchers, educators and citizens, Plateia AI aims to bridge the gap between technology and society through open dialogue, discussion and multidisciplinary collaboration.

Plateia AI thrives on community participation. Whether you're a student, a researcher, a professional or simply curious about artificial intelligence, there are many ways to get involved. For now, follow us on Instagram and LinkedIn and join our events. Soon we may also share open calls for specific projects and ideas.
